skred covers every face in a video or photo with a solid black square before you post it. Unlike blur or pixelation, a solid square cannot be undone by AI: there is no image left underneath.

<b>How it works</b>
<ul>
<li>Pick a video or photo, or share it from your gallery to skred.</li>
<li>The video is analysed 30 times per second and every face is masked on every frame.</li>
<li>You check the result: add a mask where a face was missed, remove a wrong one.</li>
<li>The file is fully rebuilt and saved to your gallery (Movies/skred or Pictures/skred). Location, date and phone model from the original file are gone.</li>
</ul>

<b>Nothing leaves your phone</b>
The app requests no permissions at all, not even internet access. No account, no analytics, no ads.

<b>What skred does not do</b>
It does not hide clothes, tattoos, voices or places. It can miss a face: always watch the whole result before posting.

<b>ethone's version</b>
This version is based on the original skred project: https://github.com/Nctrn17/skred. ethone contributes under a pseudonym, in a non-professional capacity. Contributions include moving masks frame by frame, resizing them freely, choosing their start and end times, and selecting them from the timeline. ethone is responsible for these additions, their maintenance, and security follow-up for this version. Changes made by ethone must be documented through commits on https://github.com/ethoneslop/skred.

For problems with this version, open an issue on ethone's fork: https://github.com/ethoneslop/skred/issues. Use this fork so ethone can track and handle the report directly.

The interface is in French. Face detection: YuNet model (OpenCV), run on the phone by ONNX Runtime. Source code under AGPL-3.0. The original project's website is https://skred.fr.
